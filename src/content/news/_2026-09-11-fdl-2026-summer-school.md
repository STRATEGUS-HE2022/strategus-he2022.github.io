The **FDL 2026 Summer School** was held from September 8 to 11, bringing
together PhD students, researchers, and experts for a series of invited
sessions on modeling abstractions, system engineering, cyber-physical systems,
and modern design methodologies.

The Summer School represented one of the final dissemination and
knowledge-transfer activities of **STRATEGUS**. Organizing a summer school
during the return phase of the fellowship was one of the activities originally
planned in the STRATEGUS proposal, with the goal of transferring the knowledge
developed during the project to PhD students and the wider research community.

As part of the program, Enrico Fraccaroli delivered the two-hour hands-on
tutorial **"GLACIER: An Open Framework for Modeling, Simulating, and Validating
Modern Manufacturing Systems."**

The tutorial introduced participants to the GLACIER open-source ecosystem and
its Frost simulation platform. Through practical activities, participants
explored how manufacturing machines, software components, data models, and
communication infrastructure can be represented and connected in a virtual
environment for early software validation and digital-twin development.

The Summer School provided an opportunity to bring together several themes
developed throughout STRATEGUS (modeling, simulation, deterministic
coordination, digital twins, and manufacturing-system validation) and make
them available as reusable teaching and open-source material for the research
community.
