The STRATEGUS and GLACIER teams were invited to present the **GLACIER
platform** at an official **Lingua Franca Technical Meeting**.

GLACIER is an open-source framework for designing, simulating, and validating
modern manufacturing systems. A central part of the framework is **Frost**, a
simulation environment built on Lingua Franca that uses deterministic
coordination to combine manufacturing components, communication
infrastructures, control software, and continuous-time machine models.

The presentation introduced the motivation behind GLACIER, its architecture,
the Frost simulation platform, and current applications including production
scheduling, data-driven simulation, ODE-based machine models, and digital-twin
development.

The invitation provided valuable early feedback from the Lingua Franca
community and opened new opportunities for collaborations extending beyond the
STRATEGUS project.
