The research developed in **STRATEGUS** is now being consolidated and extended
through **GLACIER**, an open-source initiative for modeling, simulating, and
validating modern manufacturing systems.

GLACIER provides a common framework around several of the ideas explored in
STRATEGUS: continuous-time equipment models, deterministic coordination,
digital-twin simulation, software validation, and integration with scheduling
and optimisation techniques.

A central component of the project is **Frost**, the simulation infrastructure
used to model manufacturing systems and validate control software before
physical deployment. Frost builds on the Lingua Franca coordination framework
to provide deterministic execution and support the integration of multiple
continuous-time models.

GLACIER is intended to remain open and reusable beyond the STRATEGUS
fellowship, allowing new case studies, research contributions, teaching
material, and industrial validation activities to build on the project's
results.
