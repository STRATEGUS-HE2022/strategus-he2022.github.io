In June 2025, as part of STRATEGUS training and knowledge-transfer activities,
Enrico Fraccaroli designed and delivered the 12-hour PhD course
**"Cyber-Physical Systems in Industry 4.0: Modeling, Networks, and
Intelligence"** at the University of Verona.

The course introduced PhD students to several research topics at the core of
STRATEGUS. It covered digital twins and different modeling strategies,
dynamics-aware optimization for flexible manufacturing, timing analysis and
communication patterns in cyber-physical systems, and distributed
intelligence for resource-constrained platforms.

The lectures combined theoretical foundations with practical case studies and
live demonstrations. STRATEGUS research results, including the **FlexMan**
scheduling approach, were used to illustrate how continuous physical dynamics
can be integrated into optimization and manufacturing-system design.

The course is part of the project's broader effort to transfer the knowledge
developed during the fellowship to a new generation of researchers and
engineers.
