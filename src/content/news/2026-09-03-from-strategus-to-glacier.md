As STRATEGUS approaches the completion of its Marie Skłodowska-Curie Global
Fellowship, several of its research results are being consolidated and extended
through **GLACIER**, an open-source initiative for modeling, simulating, and
validating modern manufacturing systems.

GLACIER is a collaborative effort involving STRATEGUS and the ICE Laboratory
at the University of Verona. It provides a common framework for many of the
ideas investigated during STRATEGUS, including structured machine models,
continuous-time physical dynamics, deterministic simulation, digital twins,
software validation, and integration with scheduling and optimization
techniques.

At the core of the ecosystem is **Frost**, an open-source simulation platform
built on Lingua Franca. Frost makes it possible to reproduce machines,
software services, communication infrastructure, and control applications
inside deterministic virtual manufacturing environments before moving to the
physical shop floor.

The platform has already been validated using the ICE Laboratory manufacturing
testbed, while additional validation and new demonstrators are being developed.

GLACIER is intended to continue beyond the STRATEGUS fellowship as an open
research infrastructure that other researchers, students, and industrial
developers can use, extend, and contribute to.
