STRATEGUS participated in the **9th Italian Workshop on Embedded Systems
(IWES 2024)**, held in Catania, Italy, on September 19–20, 2024.

The workshop brought together researchers from the Italian embedded-systems
community to discuss current research directions in embedded and
cyber-physical systems. Participation in IWES provided an opportunity to
exchange ideas around topics closely related to STRATEGUS, including system
modeling, simulation, timing, and the design of increasingly complex
cyber-physical systems.

The event was also an opportunity to strengthen connections with the broader
Italian research community while STRATEGUS was carrying out its outgoing phase
at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.
