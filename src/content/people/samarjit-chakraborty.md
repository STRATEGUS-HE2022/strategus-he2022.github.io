Samarjit Chakraborty is a William R. Kenan, Jr. Distinguished Professor in computer science at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, USA. From 2008 to 2019, he was a professor at TU Munich in Germany and an assistant professor at the National University of Singapore from 2003 to 2008. He obtained his Ph.D. in electrical engineering from ETH Zurich in 2003.

His research interests include system-level design of embedded and cyber-physical systems, with applications in automotive, healthcare and sensor networks.
